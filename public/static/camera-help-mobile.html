Camera Troubleshooting

NotReadableError: Could not start video source

This error means your camera is being blocked or is in use by another app.

📱 Mobile Phone Solutions

1. Close ALL Other Apps

Swipe up and close:

  • Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat
  • Zoom, Google Meet, WhatsApp Video
  • Facebook, Messenger
  • Camera app itself

2. Check Browser Permissions

For Chrome/Edge:

  1. Tap the or icon in address bar
  2. Find "Camera" permission
  3. Change to "Allow"
  4. Refresh page

For Safari (iOS):

  1. Go to Settings > Safari > Camera
  2. Select "Allow"
  3. Come back and refresh

3. Check Phone Settings

Android:

  1. Settings > Apps > Chrome/Browser
  2. Permissions > Camera
  3. Enable "Allow only while using app"

iPhone:

  1. Settings > Safari
  2. Camera > Ask or Allow
  3. Settings > Privacy > Camera
  4. Enable Safari

4. ⚡ Quick Fix (Works 90% of time)

  1. Close this browser completely
  2. Close ALL other apps
  3. Wait 10 seconds
  4. Open browser fresh
  5. Try again

💻 Laptop/Desktop Solutions

🪟 Windows 10/11

  1. Close Zoom, Teams, Skype, Discord
  2. Open Settings > Privacy > Camera
  3. Enable "Allow apps to access camera"
  4. Enable "Allow desktop apps to access camera"
  5. Scroll down and enable Chrome/Edge

If still not working: Open Device Manager > Cameras > Right-click camera > Enable device

🍎 macOS

  1. Close FaceTime, Zoom, Teams, Photo Booth
  2. System Preferences > Security & Privacy > Camera
  3. Check the box next to Chrome/Safari/Edge
  4. If locked , click to unlock first

📹 External USB Camera

  • Unplug camera, wait 5 seconds, plug back in
  • Try different USB port
  • Check if camera light turns on (working)
  • Update camera drivers (Windows)
  • Check cable isn't damaged

Browser-Specific Tips

Chrome

  1. Click in address bar
  2. Camera → Allow
  3. Reload page

Firefox

  1. Click in address bar
  2. Permissions → Camera
  3. Remove block, allow

Safari

  1. Safari → Preferences
  2. Websites → Camera
  3. Allow for this site

Edge

  1. Click in address bar
  2. Site permissions
  3. Camera → Allow
