Common Issues & Solutions:

  • NotAllowedError: Click the camera icon in browser address bar and allow camera access
  • NotFoundError: No camera detected - check if camera is connected/enabled
  • NotReadableError: Camera in use by another app - close Zoom, Teams, etc.
  • MediaPipe not loading: Check internet connection, try refreshing page
