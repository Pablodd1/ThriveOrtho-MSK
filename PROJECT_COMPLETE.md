## 🎓 User Guide

### **For Medical Professionals:**

**1. Patient Intake (Optional)**
- Click "Add New Patient"
- Fill in demographics
- Skip if urgent assessment needed

**2. Start Assessment**
- Click "Start Quick Assessment"
- Select camera type
- Allow camera AND microphone permissions

**3. During Assessment**
- Medical scribe activates automatically
- Speak naturally - all is transcribed
- Patient mentions pain → Alert appears
- Complete 5 exercises
- Review real-time metrics

**4. Generate Medical Note**
- Automatic SOAP note generated
- ICD-10 codes suggested
- Add/edit diagnoses
- Use MRI reader for imaging reports
- Export as PDF or print

**5. MRI Report Analysis**
- Paste report text
- Click "Analyze Report"
- Review doctor explanation
- Share patient explanation
- Add suggested ICD-10 codes
